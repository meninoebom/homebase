How it works

Write at every level.

Most journaling tools are flat. Everything lands in one pile, and every session starts from nothing.

Homebase is structured by time: life values, life goals, the year, the month, the week, today. Each level has its own page, written at its own natural cadence. Your values give shape to your year. Your year shapes your month. Your month, your week. Your week, today.

Carries forward

A fresh week opens with last week's entry, not a blank page. You refine it, update it, or replace it. But you never start from nothing.

Your folder

A journal you can grep.

There is no database and no server. Every entry is a markdown file with a plain name, sitting in a folder you chose. Open them in any editor, search them from a terminal, keep them in git, back them up the way you back up everything else.

If you stop using Homebase tomorrow, you still have your journal. That is the entire point.

Talks back

Plain markdown is also the easiest thing to hand to an assistant. Point an AI you trust at your folder and ask it what you keep circling back to. Nothing is built in and nothing phones home. Your files, your tool, your choice.
