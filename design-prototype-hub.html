Layout model: Single scrollable column, all sections visible from the start. No wizard, no tabs, no mode switching. The page IS the morning — you scroll and write wherever your eye lands. This is the Capacities template pattern that Brandon used for months.
Section anatomy: Italic serif section name (left) + roman numeral tag (right) + hairline separator between sections. Writing fields are inline — no separate "edit mode." Collapsed sections show their name in faint gray with a click-to-expand hint.
Visual hierarchy: Generative writing sections (Dreams, Inner Weather, Piano, Creative) at the top. Reference sections (Briefing, Tend) at the bottom with a different background (#FAFAFA) to signal "this is context, not your writing."
Inner Weather sub-sections: Italic prompts at 15px in muted gray, writing areas at 18px in near-black. No left borders — just vertical spacing + the italic prompt as the visual separator. Empty fields have no placeholder text (empty space is an invitation, not an obligation).
Yesterday whisper: One line at the top, below the dateline, in ghost gray with a subtle left border. Quotes Brandon's own words from yesterday's log via grep. Empty on day 1 — no "nothing to show yet" message.
Piano workspace state: The "currently working on" block has a subtle background (#FAFAFA) to distinguish standing state from today's writing. The writing field below is for today's notes.
Briefing + Tend: Both have the #FAFAFA background + smaller type + "generated" / "open tend" tags. Visually demoted: clearly reference material, not your writing. The Tend section has a highlighted "frog" (the one task you should eat first, per plan §5).
Empty vs. filled: Toggle "Fresh morning" / "Mid-morning" to see how the page fills up. On a fresh morning, Dreams has a placeholder; Inner Weather prompts are visible with empty space below; collapsed sections are a single line. Mid-morning shows content flowing in, the whisper visible, and the page gaining texture as Brandon writes.
